## ✨ Animations

### Entrance Animations
- **slideUp**: Card slides up and fades in (0.6s)
- **fadeIn**: Background fades in (0.3s)

### Continuous Animations
- **float**: Background shapes float around (20s loop)
- **pulse**: Icon wrapper pulses (2s loop)
- **bounce**: Benefit icons bounce (2s loop)

### Interaction Animations
- **Hover**: Button lifts up with shadow
- **Focus**: Input lifts and glows
- **Submit**: Spinner rotates
- **Arrow**: Moves right on hover
